### What I'm building

**Mass Apply AI** — a Mac app that finds openings on company career sites, ranks them against your profile, and fills in the application in a real browser.
Electron · React · TypeScript · SQLite · Playwright
- Pulls jobs from thousands of company job boards across the major hiring platforms, and finds new boards on its own
- Dedicated form fillers for 11 hiring platforms (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters and more), plus a page-by-page filler for everything else
- Answers come from your profile. When it isn't sure, it leaves the field blank and asks you once.
- Measured against 121 saved real application forms: 97.7% of fields filled, 0 wrong answers (as of October 2026)
- 35 releases shipped to alpha users with auto-update, and 7,400+ automated tests
